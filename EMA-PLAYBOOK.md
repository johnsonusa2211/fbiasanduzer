# 6-EMA + Pure Price Action — Quick Playbook

**Verdict:** EMAs are the filter, price action is the trigger. Never reverse it.

Preset: `5, 9, 15, 21, 100, 200`

## 1. The Stack

Long alignment:
```
5 > 9 > 15 > 21 > 100 > 200
```
Short alignment: inverse.

Valid only if:
- Fanned out, ordered, sloping same way
- No tangles / flat weaves
- 21 > 100 > 200 for clean trend regime

## 2. Roles

- **EMAs = regime:** trade or skip
- **Price Action = entry:** enter or wait

Never enter on EMA cross alone.

## 3. Best Setup: Continuation Pullback

1. Stack aligned on trade TF + HTF agrees
2. Price pulls back to fast band `5 / 9 / 15` (holds above 21/100 in uptrend)
3. Rejection print: engulfing, pin bar, inside-bar break
4. Enter on PA close/break, stop below wick / 21
5. Target: prior high/low, or trail under 15/21

HTF rule: No M15 long if H1 stack is short/flat.

## 4. Skip Filters

- **Range:** flat + tangled stack, price chopping through 5-21 — skip
- **Exhaustion:** price far from all 6 + huge engulfing — don't chase
- **News spike:** candle slices all 6 with no pullback — no PA to read, stand aside

## 5. Pre-Trade Checklist

- [ ] 5-9-15-21-100-200 stacked and fanned?
- [ ] 21 / 100 slope supports direction?
- [ ] HTF stack same direction?
- [ ] Pullback to 5/9/15, not extended?
- [ ] Clear PA rejection?
- [ ] Stop + size defined before entry?
